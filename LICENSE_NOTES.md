# Dependency licensing notes

**Checked:** 8 November 2026. Re-check immediately before production/release; package and licence terms may change.

- **Remotion 4.0.541:** source-available software under Remotion's proprietary Remotion License, not OSI open-source software. Current official pricing/FAQ says an individual or company/team of up to 3 people may qualify for the Free License, including commercial use; companies with 4+ involved people generally require a Company License unless an exemption applies. The current Company License options include Remotion for Creators (per-seat) and Remotion for Automators (per-render with a minimum spend). Eligibility depends on the exact organisation and use case; read the current terms rather than assuming that monetised content alone determines the licence category.
- **React / React DOM:** MIT.
- **React Simple Maps:** MIT (confirm from the installed package notice at release).
- **d3-geo:** ISC.
- **topojson-client:** ISC.
- **TypeScript and DefinitelyTyped declarations:** Apache-2.0 / MIT as applicable.

- https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/pricing
- https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/faq
